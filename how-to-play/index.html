How to play VocabDuel

Study each word deeply, then retrieve it in a short game. You can practise alone without an account or use anonymous Firebase authentication to enter a private battle.

Play a solo game

1. Learn the word

Open Learn to see the word, IPA, Chinese meaning and explanation, English definition, example sentences, collocations, and related words. Pronunciation uses your browser’s speech synthesis and starts only when you press a pronunciation button.

2. Choose a game

Meaning Match

Choose the correct Chinese meaning for an English word.

Reverse Match

Choose the English word that matches a Chinese prompt.

Audio Challenge

Press Listen, then identify the spoken word or its meaning.

Spell Rush

Type the English word that matches the prompt. Capitalisation and surrounding spaces do not count against you, but spelling does.

Context Challenge

Complete a sentence with the word that best fits its meaning and usage.

3. Pick a session style

Practice
No strict total timer, with immediate learning feedback.
Time Attack
Answer before the 60-second total limit expires.
Survival
Protect your remaining lives by answering accurately.
Level Challenge
Complete your chosen number of questions and aim for 80% accuracy.

4. Adjust the session

Choose 10, 15, or 20 questions, optionally add a 10–20 second question timer, and decide whether feedback waits for you or advances automatically.

5. Use keyboard controls

On multiple-choice questions, desktop players can press 1, 2, 3, or 4. Standard Tab and Enter controls remain available. Keyboard shortcuts do not override typing during Spell Rush.

Private 1v1 battles

  1. Create a room and choose the level, category, game type, question count, and round time.
  2. Share the short room code or invite link with one friend.
  3. Both players select Ready. A synchronised countdown starts when the room is ready.
  4. Answer the same questions before each deadline. Correct answers earn points, with a speed bonus for valid time remaining.
  5. Review every word and meaning, request a rematch, or leave the room.

A nickname is temporary and is not a unique account name. Multiplayer is intended for casual learning, not authoritative ranked competition.
