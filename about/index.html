About VocabDuel

VocabDuel is an English vocabulary learning game designed primarily for Chinese-speaking learners. It combines clear explanations with active recall, so a word becomes more than a translation you once saw.

Explore vocabulary

Learn deeply, practise briefly

Each curated vocabulary entry can bring together pronunciation, part of speech, a concise Chinese meaning, a fuller Chinese explanation, an English definition, examples, collocations, synonyms, and antonyms. The same vocabulary then powers focused quizzes and challenges.

That shared source of truth matters: the word you study is the word you later hear, spell, use in context, and encounter in a friendly battle.

Built around useful practice

No account required for solo learning

Study, practise, and save non-sensitive progress in your browser. You decide when to reset it.

Private battles, not public rankings

Invite one friend for a dedicated 1v1 match, or invite up to three friends to a separate Multi Duel room. Neither mode claims a global rank or cheat-proof competition.

Device-respectful pronunciation

Speech uses voices available through your browser and operating system, so voice and quality can vary by device.

A focused first release

VocabDuel is deliberately about English vocabulary: learning meanings and usage, building recall, and playing word games. It is not a broad language course, social network, or high-stakes competitive platform.
