Privacy notice

This notice explains the data used by VocabDuel’s local learning features and optional Firebase-powered multiplayer. Replace the operator and contact placeholders below before a public launch.

Local single-player data

Solo learning does not require an account. VocabDuel may store non-sensitive progress and preferences in your browser’s local storage, such as learned or difficult words, answers and accuracy, unlocked levels, recent vocabulary, local XP, theme, and pronunciation speed.

This information normally remains in that browser profile and is not synchronised to Firebase. You can remove it through VocabDuel’s Reset Progress action or your browser’s site-data controls. Clearing browser data or changing devices may permanently remove local progress.

Multiplayer data sent to Firebase

When you use multiplayer, the app communicates with Google Firebase. Anonymous Authentication creates a temporary user identifier. Realtime Database may receive that identifier, your chosen temporary nickname, room membership, ready and connection state, match configuration and timing, answer selections, and match results needed to run a private room.

Room codes are intended to be shared only with the person you invite. Do not use your legal name or other sensitive information as a nickname. Nicknames are not permanently unique usernames.

Technical data and third parties

Firebase and hosting infrastructure may process network and device information such as IP address, browser information, request logs, and security signals to provide, protect, and diagnose the service. Their processing is governed by the applicable service provider terms and configuration selected by the site operator.

Firebase App Check may be enabled to help reject requests from unauthorised app clients. App Check is an abuse-reduction control, not a way to identify you personally and not a guarantee against abuse.

Purpose, retention, and sharing

Data is used to provide learning features, operate rooms, synchronise matches, maintain security, and troubleshoot failures. Multiplayer room data should be removed after a limited operational period through configured cleanup. Until an automated cleanup service is deployed, the operator must periodically delete expired rooms.

VocabDuel does not require sensitive personal information for normal use. Data is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the website, or when legally required. This initial notice does not claim that advertising or analytics are active.

Your choices

  • Use solo learning without enabling multiplayer.
  • Choose a nickname that does not identify you.
  • Reset locally stored progress from the app or browser.
  • Leave a multiplayer room to end active participation.

Children, changes, and contact

The site is not designed to collect children’s personal details. A production operator should evaluate age and consent requirements for every launch region before offering the service to children.

This notice may change when features or service providers change. The updated date will be revised when that happens.
