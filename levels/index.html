IELTS and MUET guidance

These ranges are helpful starting points—not official bands or score guarantees. IELTS and MUET assess listening, speaking, reading, and writing; vocabulary is only one part of your overall performance.

How levels work

Learn the meanings and examples, then reinforce them in short games. Move between levels whenever the words feel too easy or too difficult.
