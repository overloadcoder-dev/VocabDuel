A path that grows with you

Five vocabulary levels, one clear progression

VocabDuel’s levels organize practice by familiarity and language complexity. They are an internal learning guide—not a claim of exact equivalence with an official exam or CEFR band.

Start learning

Choose your starting level

Level 1

Basic

Common words for everyday people, places, actions, and needs. Start here if you want to strengthen essential recall.

Level 2

Elementary

Frequently encountered words that require more precise meaning and slightly stronger spelling or context skills.

Level 3

Intermediate

More abstract vocabulary found in school, articles, work, and thoughtful everyday conversation.

Level 4

Upper intermediate

Less common words, nuanced meanings, and more demanding context for confident independent learners.

Level 5

Advanced

Academic, professional, and nuanced vocabulary where usage and collocation matter as much as translation.

How levels work in practice

Filter words by level and category, learn their Chinese explanations and English examples, then reinforce them in short games. You can move between levels at any time. A harder level is not always better: practice where the words feel challenging but still learnable.

Optional CEFR labels may appear only where the vocabulary has been accurately curated. VocabDuel’s Level 1–5 system remains its own progression.
