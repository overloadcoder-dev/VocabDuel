How to read the IELTS guidance

These ranges are orientation labels, not official IELTS vocabulary bands or score guarantees. IELTS assesses how accurately, appropriately, and flexibly you use vocabulary in context—alongside listening, reading, writing, speaking, grammar, fluency, and other skills. Knowing Level 4 words does not by itself mean that you will receive Band 7.

How levels work in practice

Filter words by level and category, learn their Chinese explanations and English examples, then reinforce them in short games. You can move between levels at any time. A harder level is not always better: practise where the words feel challenging but still learnable.

Levels consider frequency, abstractness, range of meanings, collocation, register, and the precision needed to use a word naturally.
